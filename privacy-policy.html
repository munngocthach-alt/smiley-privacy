Privacy Policy — Smiley II

Effective date: 7 October 2026
Developer: thestone ("we", "us")
Contact: gamevui.thestone@gmail.com

1. What Smiley II is

Smiley II is a single-player jumping game for Android, iPhone and iPad. It is not directed at children under 13, and it is designed to work without collecting personal information from anyone who plays it.

2. Information the game itself collects

None.

  • Smiley II has no accounts, no sign-in, no user names and no way to enter personal details.
  • Your progress and your settings (music and sound volume) are stored only on your device,

in the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

  • The game works fully offline. It does not need an internet connection to play.
  • The game does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other device sensor,

and requests no permissions for them.

3. Advertising

The Main Menu can show one small banner advertisement, and a full-screen advertisement may appear when you press Continue or New Game before the climb begins, or when you open the menu during a climb — at most once every few minutes, and it can always be closed. Adverts never appear during play itself.

You can remove all advertising permanently with the Remove Ads purchase on the Main Menu. Once bought, no banner and no full-screen advert is shown again, and the advertising service described below is not started at all.

Adverts are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), an advertising service operated by Unity Technologies. We configure the service so that it is given **no consent for personalised advertising and is told not to sell** personal information. As a result, adverts shown in Smiley II are contextual, not personalised — they are not based on a profile of you. Smiley II does not show a consent dialog because it never asks for, and never uses, personalised advertising.

To deliver an advert, the advertising service processes a limited amount of technical information from the device, such as the IP address, device type and operating system version, and a non-persistent identifier for the advertising session, for the purposes of delivering the advert, measuring that it was shown, and preventing fraud. This information is handled by Unity Technologies under its own privacy policy, which you can read at:

  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource privacy information: https://unity.com/legal/game-solutions/privacy-policy

We do not receive personal information from the advertising service; we receive only aggregated reports (for example, how many adverts were shown).

No other advertising networks are integrated into Smiley II.

3a. The Remove Ads purchase

If you buy Remove Ads, the purchase is handled entirely by the store you installed the game from — Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPhone and iPad. We never see your payment details: the store processes the payment and tells the app only that the purchase exists. Each store's handling of that transaction is covered by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

The app remembers your purchase on your device, and asks the store each time it starts whether this account owns it — which is how the purchase comes back if you reinstall the game or change phone. We keep no record of it ourselves, because we have no server.

3b. Tracking, on Apple devices

On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks you a question the first time you open the game: whether Smiley II may track you across other companies' apps and websites. That question is Apple's, not ours, and we only ever see the answer.

  • If you say no, nothing changes about the game, and adverts still appear. They are simply not

personalised.

  • If you say yes, the advertising service may use your device's advertising identifier to make

adverts more relevant.

Either way the game plays identically, and either way we keep nothing about you ourselves. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

ⓘ On Android there is no such prompt, because Android has no equivalent of it. Adverts there are not personalised either.

4. Children's privacy

Smiley II is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game is built so that it has nothing to collect, and the advertising service described in Section 3 is configured never to personalise adverts for anyone.

If you believe that personal information has been collected from a child through Smiley II, please contact us at gamevui.thestone@gmail.com and we will investigate and delete it.

5. Your choices

  • Delete all local data: Settings → Reset Game deletes your progress; uninstalling the app

deletes everything the app stored on the device.

  • Advertising settings on your device: Android's system settings let you reset or opt out of

ads personalisation for the whole device; Smiley II does not use personalised adverts in any case.

  • Adverts in general: playing offline (aeroplane mode) means no advert can load; the game is

unaffected.

  • Remove all adverts: the Remove Ads purchase on the Main Menu, which also stops the

advertising service from starting.

6. Data retention

The game keeps your progress and settings on your device for as long as the app is installed. We keep nothing on any server, because nothing is sent to one. Retention of the technical information processed by the advertising service is governed by Unity's policy linked above.

7. Security

Data stored by the game stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Because we hold no personal data on servers, there is no server-side data to breach.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what Smiley II collects or how adverts work, we will update this policy and the effective date above before the change reaches players, and the app store listing will link to the current version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your privacy: gamevui.thestone@gmail.com.
